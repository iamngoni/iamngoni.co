what if i’m just a social experiment—
a person placed among people
to see how much love he can give
before he realises
none of it was ever his to keep?

maybe every silence was recorded.
every goodbye, a result.
every i’m not going anywhere
just another variable
changed halfway through the trial.

maybe they wanted to know
how many times a heart could be disappointed
and still answer gently.
how often someone could be forgotten
and still remember everyone.
how long a person could keep saying
i understand
before finally admitting
that he didn’t.

maybe i was never difficult to love.
maybe love was never the question.

maybe the question was
how much of myself i would abandon
just to stop other people
from leaving.

and maybe this ends
the moment i become aware—

the subject stops performing.
the subject chooses himself.
the subject leaves the experiment.
