title: Tomorrow
date: 2026-11-06
description: On the eve of becoming a man — not by age, but by responsibility, sacrifice and the willingness to love beyond yourself.
---

Tomorrow,

there’s a chance that after tomorrow, I will be a man.

Well, I’ve always been one, gender-wise. 😂
But I mean the proper transition. From being a boy to becoming a man.

Oh man, Apostle Paul!
I’ve never seen the man, but goodness, did he know how to put things into perspective.

“When I was a child, I talked like a child, I thought like a child, I reasoned like a child. When I became a man, I put the ways of childhood behind me.”
— 1 Corinthians 13:11

The transition I’ve looked forward to since I was a child.
The day I get to look at myself and acknowledge that I’ve stepped into a new chapter of my life.

Not because everything suddenly makes sense.
Not because I have all the answers.
But because I’m beginning to understand that being a man is about so much more than age.

It’s about responsibility.
Sacrifice.
Intentionality.
Accountability.
And, above all, the willingness to love beyond yourself.

And tomorrow, I get to take another step towards that.

I think about the little boy I once was —
the things he dreamed about,
the man he hoped to become —
and I can’t help but smile.

There’s still so much growing to do,
so much to learn,
so much life to experience.

But goodness, what a privilege it is to get here.

And if there’s one thing I hope to carry into this next chapter,
it’s this:

becoming a man isn’t about losing the boy I once was.

It’s about honouring him,
by becoming everything he dreamed of,
and perhaps even more.

Here’s to growing up,
growing wiser,
and becoming a man worthy of the life, the love and the responsibilities entrusted to him.

Tomorrow, we grow. ❤️
